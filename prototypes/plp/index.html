Frequently Asked Questions

How many bikes can I carry, and does it depend on my vehicle?

It depends on the mounting style, not your vehicle — roof-mounted wheel-support carriers typically carry 1 bike per unit (fit multiple for more), while tow bar platform racks carry 2 to 4 bikes on one unit. Use the "How many bikes" filter to narrow the range quickly.

What's the difference between a hang-on and a platform bike rack?

Hang-on racks support the bike by its frame; platform racks support it by the wheels on a tray. Platform racks are generally easier to load and gentler on the bike's frame, but sit further back off the vehicle.

Do I need roof bars already fitted to use a roof-mounted bike rack?

Most roof-mounted carriers clamp onto an existing roof bar or factory roof rail — check the "Vehicle Fit Type" filter for Clamp Mount vs. Track Mount options, or use Fit My Vehicle to confirm what your roof already has.

Can e-bikes go on any bike rack?

No — e-bikes are heavier, so they need a carrier rated for the extra weight. Filter by "Bike Racks for E-Bikes" under Type of Carrier, or check the Product Load Rating filter.
