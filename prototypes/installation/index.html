Roof Rack Installation & Fitting

Roof rack installation has become more complicated, and it's easy to cause damage, leaks or rust without the right know-how. Vehicle roofs are thinner and weaker than they used to be, and harder to hold onto because of their shapes.

With over 30 years of experience, our installers fit roof racks, bike racks, roof boxes, platforms and more every day, and every installation is covered by our warranty.

How It Works

  1. Choose Your Setup

    Buy online or in store. Use Fit My Vehicle to find exactly what fits.

  2. Book a Fitting

    Send a request below and your store will confirm a time, or just drop by.

  3. Fitted & Tested

    Our installers mount, torque and load-test your rack, so it leaves safe and ready to go.

How long does it take? Anything from 30 minutes for a basic removable rack to 8 hours for an extremely complicated fit-out. It depends on the products and your vehicle, so check with your local store for advice on your fitment. Most fittings are done while you wait.
