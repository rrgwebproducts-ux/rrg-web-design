Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a fitting take?

Anything from 30 minutes for a basic removable rack to 8 hours for a complicated fit-out. It depends on the products and your vehicle, so your store will give you a time when they confirm your booking.

Can I wait in store while it’s fitted?

Yes. Most fittings are done while you wait, and you’re welcome to look around the showroom in the meantime. If you’d like to see how it goes on, just ask the team.

Do I need to leave my car with you?

Usually not. Bigger jobs like platforms, roller shutters or van fit-outs can take longer, and your store will let you know if it’s easier to drop the car off.

Can you fit a rack I bought somewhere else?

Yes, we’re happy to fit it. There’s a surcharge for products not bought from Roof Racks Galore, and your store will confirm it when they book you in.

Do you fit accessories as well as roof racks?

Yes. Bike racks, roof boxes, awnings, roof top tents, roller shutters, ladder racks and more. If it’s not on the list, ask your nearest store and they’ll let you know.

What if my vehicle needs extra work?

Some vehicles need roof rails removed or extra parts before a rack can go on. Your store checks your vehicle and tells you before any extra work starts.
