What Roof Racks Fit My Ford Ranger?

The short answer: it depends on your Ranger's generation and roof type, not just the model name. A Next-Gen Wildtrak with factory rails and a PX1 base-model with a bare roof need completely different mounting systems — even though they're both "Ford Rangers". Get the roof type wrong and you're looking at a rack that either won't fit or won't carry its rated load safely.

Why fitment varies on the Ranger

Ford changed the Ranger's roof design across generations, and trim level matters too — not every Ranger left the factory with rails fitted:

  • Factory roof rails (raised): Common on higher trims through the PX series. These give you a raised mounting point, which usually means a leg-based or clip-mount rack system.
  • Factory roof rails (flush/low-profile): More common on the Next-Gen Ranger, particularly on Wildtrak and higher grades. These sit close to the roofline and need a track-mount or specific flush-rail clamp system.
  • No rails — bare roof: Base-trim Rangers across most generations (and some Next-Gen XL/XLS variants) come with no rails at all. These need a fixed-point or track system that mounts directly to the roof panel, installed by a qualified fitter.

What we always tell customers

The single most common mistake we see with Ranger roof rack orders is buyers assuming their ute has factory rails because "most Rangers do." Plenty of base and mid-spec Rangers across every generation left the factory with a bare roof. If you're not certain, send us a photo of your roofline before you order — it takes seconds to confirm and saves you the cost of returning the wrong rack.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the common questions our team hears everyday regarding Roof Racks for the Ford Ranger.

Here you'll also find answers to some of the most common questions we receive about our product range, including fitment, installation, load capacity, and general product use. If you can't find what you're looking for here, our team is always happy to help — simply get in touch with us on 1300 071 264 and we'll be glad to assist with any additional queries.

Do I need an adapter for the 2022+ Ranger's raised roof rails?

No — the 2022+ Ranger's factory raised roof rails accept most crossbar systems and platform racks directly. Products marked "Raised Roof Rail" on our site are ready to fit without additional hardware.

What's the maximum weight I can carry on a Ranger roof rack?

Load rating depends on whether your Ranger has factory rails or a fixed-point/track system — a bare-roof fitment rated for a Backbone-style system typically carries more than a factory-rail system. Check the specific product's rated load and never exceed your vehicle manufacturer's own roof load limit, whichever is lower.

Can I still open the sunroof with a roof rack fitted?

In most cases yes — our platform and leg-based systems are designed to clear a sunroof opening. If you're fitting a full-length platform, let us know your cab configuration when ordering and we'll confirm clearance.

Do you offer professional installation for the Ford Ranger?

Yes — we fit roof racks nationwide at our 35+ stores. Book an installation above or use the Store Finder to find your nearest fitting centre.

How do I clean and maintain my roof rack on my Ranger?

Regularly rinse off salt/road grime with fresh water, check mounting bolts are still torqued to spec every few months, and inspect rubber feet/clamps for wear if you remove and refit the rack often.

Do roof racks affect fuel economy in my Ranger?

Yes, to some degree — added wind resistance typically increases highway fuel consumption slightly, more so with a roof box or bulky cargo fitted than with a bare platform or set of bars.

Can I install a Ranger roof rack myself?

Most factory-rail-mounted systems are a straightforward DIY fit with basic tools. Bare-roof/fixed-point systems involve drilling and torque-critical mounting, so we recommend professional installation for those.

Are roof racks universal or Ranger-specific?

Ranger-specific. Mounting points, leg spacing and load ratings are engineered per generation and roof type — use the Fit Finder above to confirm exact compatibility before ordering.

What weight can a Ranger roof rack hold?

This varies by product and mounting system — see the individual product's specifications for its dynamic (driving) and static (stationary) load ratings.
