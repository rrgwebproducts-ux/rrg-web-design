Product Information

The Yakima RoadShower 15L Complete Shower & Hose Bundle takes the versatility of the RoadShower to the next level, creating an all-in-one pressurised water system ready for both showering and cleaning.

At the heart of the bundle is the Yakima RoadShower 15L Small (8004109), a roof rack-mounted pressurised water tank constructed from welded aluminium with a durable powder-coated finish. The tank features two water outlet ports – one positioned at each end – allowing you to configure your water setup to suit your adventure. This bundle takes advantage of both outlets.

By adding the included second Yakima RoadShower On-Off Elbow (8004103), you can have a dedicated accessory connected at either end of the RoadShower. Yakima specifically designed the additional elbow to activate the second 1/2" outlet port and make it easier to run multiple RoadShower accessories.

Set one end up with the 214cm Extra Long Hose for rinsing:

  • Muddy bikes
  • Dirty camping gear
  • Sandy feet
  • Pets
  • Wetsuits and water sports equipment
  • Campsite dishes
  • Recovery equipment
  • General outdoor gear

Then leave the 76cm FlexHead Shower connected at the opposite end, giving you a dedicated shower whenever it's time to wash off after a day on the tracks, trails or beach.

The result is a much more convenient RoadShower setup. Instead of disconnecting the hose every time you want to attach the shower head, both accessories can remain installed and ready for use.

The 214cm Extra Long Hose provides substantially more reach around the vehicle and connects to the On-Off Elbow using quick-connect hardware. This makes it ideal for washing larger equipment or reaching muddy bikes and gear without having to position everything immediately beside the vehicle.

At the other end, the 76cm FlexHead Shower features a flexible neck and variable shower head, turning the RoadShower into a convenient outdoor shower for camping, touring and remote adventures. It also connects directly to the On-Off Elbow using quick-connect hardware.

The RoadShower itself can be pressurised through its Schrader air valve using a hand or electric pump. Its built-in pressure relief valve automatically limits pressure to a maximum of 65psi, providing a useful pressurised water supply even when you're well away from mains water.

During the day, the black aluminium tank naturally absorbs heat from the sun, helping warm the stored water for a more comfortable shower later at camp. A stick-on thermometer strip lets you check the tank temperature before use.

The RoadShower mounts securely to compatible roof racks using stainless steel universal mounting hardware and includes SKS Locks to help secure the unit to the vehicle.

Whether you're washing the mud from your mountain bike, rinsing sand from your feet, cleaning the dog or having a shower at the end of the day, this bundle gives you a dedicated hose and dedicated shower setup in one complete system.
