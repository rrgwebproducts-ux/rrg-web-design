Rhino Rack 62100 Platform

The Rhino-Rack 6 Series Pioneer Platform delivers a strong, lightweight, and highly versatile roof cargo solution designed for touring, work, and everyday transport. Built to maximise carrying capability without compromising vehicle performance, this platform provides a stable base for mounting accessories, luggage, and outdoor equipment with confidence.

Engineered using advanced materials and an aerodynamic design, the Rhino Rack 62100 Platform helps reduce wind drag and noise vs other roof rack options while maintaining exceptional durability and strength. The refined configuration offers increased usable surface area and more mounting channels, making it easier to attach awnings, recovery gear, bike carriers, roof boxes, and camping equipment.

The low-profile design keeps the load close to the roofline for improved stability and vehicle handling. Integrated wiring capabilities allow clean installation of lighting and electrical accessories, while the corrosion-resistant finish ensures long-term performance in harsh conditions.

Suitable for a wide range of vehicles when paired with the correct fitting system, the Pioneer platform is ideal for overland travel, trade applications, and family adventures. Its modular design supports a huge range of compatible accessories, allowing the setup to evolve as requirements change.

Key Features and Benefits

  • Lightweight yet heavy-duty construction for reliable load carrying
  • Aerodynamic design reduces drag, wind noise, and fuel impact
  • More accessory channels for flexible mounting options
  • Increased usable platform area compared with previous generations
  • Integrated provision for wiring and lighting accessories
  • Corrosion-resistant finish for all-weather durability
  • Low-profile fit for improved vehicle stability and clearance
  • Compatible with a wide range of Rhino-Rack accessories

Typical Applications

  • Touring and overland setups
  • Trade and commercial equipment transport
  • Mounting roof tents and awnings
  • Carrying recovery boards and jerry cans
  • Bike, kayak, and water sports carriers
  • General luggage and bulky cargo

Rhino Rack RTHB1 Platform Backbone

  • Stylishly designed to suit rugged off-road conditions

RTHB1 suits Pioneer Platform 52100 for both the N70 & N80 Hilux and requires mounting to a Rhino-Rack RTS track set. Additionally, the 45100B and 41100 trays are also compatible.

Rhino Rack RTS556 Tracks

The Rhino-Rack RTS556 Tracks are designed specifically for the Toyota Hilux Double Cab (2015-Current), providing a secure and reliable base for mounting roof rack systems.

Manufactured from high-grade aluminium with a durable powder-coated finish, these rivet-in tracks replace the factory roof trim and allow flexible bar or platform positioning along the track length. Ideal for both work and adventure applications, the RTS556 system delivers maximum strength and corrosion resistance to withstand tough Australian conditions.

Supplied as a pair with all required hardware and end caps, these tracks are compatible with Rhino-Rack Pioneer Platforms and Vortex or Heavy Duty bar systems. Once installed, they create a low-profile, factory-style fitment that supports a wide range of accessories for carrying tools, gear, or recreational equipment.

  • Vehicle-specific design for Toyota Hilux Double Cab (2015-Current)
  • Heavy-duty aluminium construction for strength and durability
  • Rivet-in installation replaces factory roof trim
  • Compatible with Rhino-Rack Pioneer Platforms and roof rack systems
  • Includes hardware and end caps for a complete fitment
  • Tested to industry standards for load and safety performance
