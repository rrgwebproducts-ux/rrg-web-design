Product Information

The Thule Motion 3 L provides 450L of internal storage in a sleek, aerodynamic package. It's designed to carry gear and luggage without adding unnecessary bulk. The box features PowerClick quick-mount clamps that click when properly torqued, DualSide opening for access from either side, and a SlideLock system that locks automatically and indicates when sealed. Its refined construction and design make it ideal for everyday use and longer journeys.

  • Internal volume: 450L
  • Uses PowerClick clamps for quick and reliable mounting
  • DualSide lid opening allows access from either side of vehicle
  • SlideLock locking mechanism secures the lid and confirms closure
  • Elegant, aerodynamic shell designed for durability and looks
  • Ideal balance of capacity, usability, and aesthetics

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the Motion 3 L fit my roof bars?

It mounts with Thule PowerClick clamps and suits crossbars spaced between 555mm and 930mm apart. Measure the gap between your bars, or ask in store and we'll check your setup.

How many skis or snowboards will it carry?

5 to 7 pairs of skis up to 1800mm long, or 3 to 5 snowboards.

Does it lock?

Yes. Locks are included, the SlideLock system locks the lid automatically and shows when it's sealed, and it works with Thule's One Key System.

Is there any difference between the colours?

No. Gloss Black and Gloss Titan share the same shell, 450L volume, 75kg load rating and fittings; colour is personal preference.
