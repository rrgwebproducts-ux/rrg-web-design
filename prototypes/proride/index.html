Product Information

The Thule ProRide 598 Black delivers fast and secure upright bike transport for roof racks, designed to carry bicycles up to 20 kg with minimal effort. With automatic positioning, torque-controlled clamping and wheel stabilisation, it is a dependable solution for everyday cycling transport, road trips and weekend adventures.

  • Upright design: the frame holder aligns the bike automatically during loading
  • Torque-limited clamp: a clear click signals the right force, reducing the risk of frame damage
  • Wheel trays and diagonal quick-release straps keep the bike stable on long journeys
  • Tool-free installation on compatible roof crossbars, and swaps between vehicle sides without tools
  • Locks the bike to the carrier and the carrier to the roof rack; locking system included

Vehicle compatibility. Compatible with most roof racks featuring standard crossbars. Suits bicycles with frame dimensions between 22–80 mm round or 80–100 mm oval, wheel sizes from 16 to 32 inches, and tyre widths up to 3 inches. Some bar types need an adapter: see the Compatibility tab.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum bike weight the Thule ProRide 598 supports?

It supports bicycles up to 20 kg, making it suitable for most road, hybrid and mountain bikes.

Does this bike carrier require tools for installation?

No. It features a tool-free mounting system designed for quick installation on compatible roof racks.

Can the carrier be mounted on either side of the vehicle?

Yes. The design allows switching between left and right mounting sides without tools.

Is the bike secured to the carrier during transport?

Yes. The system includes frame and wheel locking points, and a locking system is included for added security.
