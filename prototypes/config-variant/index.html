Frequently Asked Questions

What's the actual difference between Assembled and Flat Pack?

The platform and hardware are identical — Assembled arrives pre-built and ready to bolt straight to your crossbars, Flat Pack arrives disassembled at a lower price and takes roughly 30–45 minutes to build using the included instructions.

Does the plank count really differ between the two options?

Our current data shows a difference in plank count between the two builds — see the Specifications tab for the exact count on your selected option. We're confirming with the manufacturer whether this is intentional.

What weight can the platform carry?

See the Specifications tab for this SKU's rated load capacity — this varies by vehicle roof-load rating as well as the platform itself, so always check your vehicle's own limit too.

Can I get this professionally fitted?

Yes — select the "Get It Fitted" option where shown, or book fitting in-store after purchase. See the Gold Guarantee tab for what's covered when we fit it for you.
