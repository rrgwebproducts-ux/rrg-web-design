Product Information

This Prorack roof rack package includes everything you need to fit to your Volkswagen Amarok Gen 2 4dr Ute (2023 to 2027) with Raised Roof Rail.

Prorack P17 Roof Bars with pre-fitted Legs

The Prorack P-Bar system pairs square through-bars with Smartfoot legs, built-in locks and adjustable legs. An affordable, easy-to-fit aluminium rack rated to around 75kg.

Prorack / Yakima K328 Fitting Kit

The vehicle-specific fitting kit that clamps the P-Bar legs onto the Amarok Gen 2's raised roof rails.
