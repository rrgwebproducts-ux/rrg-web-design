Roof Rack installation has become more complicated and risky to install without causing damage, leaking and rust issues. The main reason for this is vehicle roofs have become weaker, thinner sheet metal and more difficult to hold on due to vehicle shapes. With over 30 years of experience Roof Racks Galore are the industry leading experts when it comes to Roof Racks & Bike Racks and are the best choice when it comes to roof rack installs. All our installations are covered by our extensive warranty and we guarantee to provide the best roof rack installation service.

Due to the complexities of racks on today's cars installations can range from 30mins for a basic removable rack through to 8 hours for an extremely complicated fit out. Depending on the products and vehicle your fit out time will vary so please contact your local store for advice on your fitment.
