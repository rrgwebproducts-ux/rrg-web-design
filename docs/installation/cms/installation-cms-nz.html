Roof Rack Installation FAQs

How much does roof rack installation cost in NZ?

Roof rack installation costs in New Zealand vary depending on your vehicle, the type of roof rack system and whether drilling or additional labour is required. Basic removable roof rack fitting starts from the lower end of our pricing guide, while more complex platform and permanent fitments cost more.

How long does roof rack fitting take?

Installation time depends on your vehicle and the product being fitted. Some removable roof rack systems can be installed in as little as 30 minutes, while more complex fitouts such as platforms, van racks or permanent systems may take several hours.

Do I need professional roof rack installation?

Professional installation is recommended because modern vehicles often require precise fitment. A trained installer can reduce the risk of incorrect mounting, leaks, rust, wind noise and damage to your vehicle.
